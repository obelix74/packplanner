PACK LIKE A PRO WITH PACKPLANNER

PackPlanner is the essential companion for hikers, backpackers, and outdoor enthusiasts who want to optimize their gear and reduce pack weight. Whether you're planning a weekend camping trip or a months-long thru-hike, PackPlanner helps you organize, weigh, and perfect your loadout.

KEY FEATURES:

⚖️ SMART WEIGHT TRACKING
• Track base weight, worn weight, and consumables separately
• See exactly where every ounce goes
• Optimize your pack for maximum comfort and efficiency
• Real-time weight calculations that update instantly

📋 COMPREHENSIVE GEAR LIBRARY
• Build your complete gear inventory with detailed information
• Add weights, categories, and custom descriptions
• Organize by Shelter, Sleep System, Cooking, Clothing, and more
• Never forget an essential item again

🏔️ TRIP PLANNING MADE EASY
• Create custom gear lists for each adventure
• Toggle items as worn, consumable, or verified
• Filter by pending items to see what needs attention
• Copy hikes to reuse gear lists for similar trips

🔄 IMPERIAL & METRIC SUPPORT
• Seamlessly switch between pounds/ounces and kilograms/grams
• Perfect for international hiking adventures
• All weights displayed in your preferred unit

📊 DETAILED WEIGHT BREAKDOWN
• Base Weight - Essential non-consumable gear
• Worn Weight - Items worn on your body
• Consumable Weight - Food, fuel, and toiletries
• Total Pack Weight - Complete weight analysis

☁️ ICLOUD SYNC
• Access your gear lists across all Apple devices
• Automatic backup to iCloud
• Your data stays in sync seamlessly

🎨 MODERN, INTUITIVE DESIGN
• Beautiful SwiftUI interface optimized for iOS
• Smooth animations and responsive controls
• Easy-to-use swipe actions for quick edits
• Dark mode support

📤 EXPORT & SHARE
• Export gear lists to CSV format
• Share packing lists with hiking partners
• Backup your data externally

PERFECT FOR:
• Day hikers planning weekend adventures
• Thru-hikers optimizing for ultralight setups
• Backpackers tracking gear across multiple trips
• Outdoor enthusiasts building their gear collection
• Anyone who wants organized, lightweight packing

WHY HIKERS LOVE PACKPLANNER:
"Finally, a gear app that actually helps me pack lighter!" - Trail enthusiast
"The weight breakdown feature is a game-changer for planning" - Backpacker
"Clean interface, works offline, does exactly what I need" - Thru-hiker

GETTING STARTED:
1. Add your gear items with weights and categories
2. Create a hike and add gear to your pack list

4. Export or share your perfect packing list
